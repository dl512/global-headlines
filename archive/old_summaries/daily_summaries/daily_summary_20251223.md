## Most Important Stories

- **Trump's Greenland Ambitions**: US President Donald Trump has reiterated the importance of acquiring Greenland, stating that "the US has to have" the territory, and appointed a special envoy to facilitate discussions, despite pushback from Greenland's Prime Minister.

- **Laos Ferry Disaster**: A ferry capsized in the Mekong River in Laos, leading to chaos and the tragic loss of lives, including a woman and her one-year-old child. The incident has raised serious concerns about passenger safety, as the ferry reportedly had insufficient life jackets.

- **US Military Strikes ISIS in Syria**: The US military launched significant strikes against ISIS targets in Syria, termed "OPERATION HAWKEYE STRIKE," in retaliation for an attack that killed US personnel. The operation involved over 70 targets and was supported by Jordanian forces.

- **Justice Department Sues DC Over Gun Laws**: The US Justice Department has filed a lawsuit against Washington, DC, challenging its ban on the registration of semi-automatic firearms, including the AR-15, arguing that it infringes on Second Amendment rights.

- **Amazon Blocks North Korean Job Applications**: Amazon has blocked over 1,800 job applications from suspected North Korean agents attempting to secure remote IT positions, highlighting concerns about sophisticated fraud tactics aimed at funding North Korea's weapons programs.

- **Zelenskyy Supports US-led Talks**: Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy expressed support for a US proposal for three-way talks involving the US, Ukraine, and Russia, contingent on the talks producing results, particularly regarding prisoner exchanges.

- **EU Approves Aid for Ukraine**: The European Union has approved a substantial $105.5 billion aid package for Ukraine for 2026 and 2027, emphasizing the importance of financial security amidst ongoing conflict with Russia.

- **Epstein Files Release Sparks Controversy**: The release of Jeffrey Epstein files by the Department of Justice has drawn criticism for being incomplete, with Senate Democrats accusing the Trump administration of a cover-up to protect US President Donald Trump.

- **Trump's New Battleship Initiative**: US President Donald Trump announced plans for a new fleet of battleships named the "Golden Fleet," aimed at revitalizing American shipbuilding in response to concerns about naval capacity compared to China.

- **Senator Lummis Announces Retirement**: Senator Cynthia Lummis, a key advocate for the cryptocurrency industry, announced her retirement, raising concerns among crypto supporters about the future of legislation regulating the industry.

- **Kremlin Dismisses Peace Proposal Changes**: The Kremlin has stated that recent changes to peace proposals by European and Ukrainian negotiators have not improved the prospects for resolving the ongoing conflict in Ukraine, emphasizing skepticism about their effectiveness.

- **Trump Tariffs Burden Small Businesses**: New tariffs imposed by US President Donald Trump have significantly raised operating costs for small businesses, forcing many to seek high-interest loans, raising fears of financial instability even if the tariffs are later deemed illegal.
