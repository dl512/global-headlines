## Most Important Stories

- **Trump Orders Blockade of Venezuelan Oil Tankers**: US President Donald Trump has announced a "total and complete" blockade of sanctioned oil tankers entering and leaving Venezuela, labeling the Maduro government as a terrorist regime involved in drug smuggling and human trafficking. This move follows increased military presence in the Caribbean and has been condemned by Venezuela as a violation of international law.

- **Ukraine Faces Energy Crisis Amid Russian Attacks**: Ukraine is struggling with severe power shortages due to ongoing Russian attacks on its energy infrastructure, leading to electricity rationing and prolonged outages. The situation is critical as winter approaches, with officials warning that the cold could be weaponized against the population.

- **Mass Shooting in Australia**: The first funerals for victims of a mass shooting at Bondi Beach in Australia have taken place, with 21 individuals still hospitalized, including one in critical condition. The incident has drawn international attention, including expressions of solidarity from US President Donald Trump.

- **Nick Reiner Charged with Parents' Murder**: Nick Reiner, son of Hollywood director Rob Reiner, has been charged with two counts of first-degree murder in the deaths of his parents. The alleged weapon was a knife, and if convicted, he faces a maximum sentence of life in prison without parole.

- **Trump's Chief of Staff Disputes Vanity Fair Claims**: US President Donald Trump's chief of staff, Susie Wiles, has refuted claims made in a Vanity Fair article that criticized Trump and other figures in his administration, describing him as having an "alcoholic's personality." The article has sparked discussions about the dynamics within Trump's inner circle.

- **Military Build-Up in the Caribbean**: The US is experiencing its largest military build-up in the Caribbean since the Cold War, driven by tensions with Venezuela. This situation has drawn parallels to the 1989 invasion of Panama, with President Trump linking Maduro to alleged drug trafficking.

- **Trump's Address to the Nation**: US President Donald Trump is scheduled to deliver a live address to the nation, where he will highlight what he describes as "historic accomplishments" and may outline upcoming policies for the new year.

- **Lawsuit Over Withheld EV Funds**: Sixteen states and the District of Columbia have filed a lawsuit against President Trump's administration for allegedly withholding over $2 billion in funding for electric vehicle charging programs, claiming it obstructs the bipartisan infrastructure law.

- **Dinosaur Footprints Discovered in Italy**: Thousands of dinosaur footprints dating back 210 million years have been found in Stelvio National Park, Italy. The discovery highlights the region's rich paleontological history and will be studied using drones due to its remote location.
