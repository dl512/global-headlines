## Most Important Stories

- **Australia announces gun buyback scheme**: In response to a deadly mass shooting at Bondi Beach that claimed 15 lives, Australia is implementing a gun buyback initiative, the largest since the 1996 Port Arthur massacre, alongside stricter gun control measures.

- **Brown University shooting suspect found dead**: The primary suspect in a mass shooting at Brown University, which resulted in two deaths and nine injuries, has been found dead in New Hampshire. Authorities confirmed he acted alone and had no current ties to the university.

- **TikTok owner signs agreements to avoid US ban**: ByteDance, the owner of TikTok, has entered into binding agreements with US investors to form a joint venture, aiming to mitigate national security concerns and prevent a ban on the app in the United States.

- **US trade demands from Canada**: US trade representative Jamieson Greer outlined key demands for Canada to maintain free trade, including opening its dairy market and revising the Online Streaming Act, amid ongoing discussions about the USMCA.

- **Bangladesh unrest over student leader's death**: Protests have erupted across Bangladesh following the death of a student leader, leading to clashes with security forces and highlighting ongoing tensions related to political repression and student activism.

- **EU finalizes $105 billion Ukraine funding deal**: The European Union has reached a significant funding agreement to support Ukraine amid its conflict with Russia, notably deciding not to use frozen Russian assets for this purpose at this time.

- **Putin rejects compromise on Ukraine**: Russian President Vladimir Putin has dismissed any possibility of compromise regarding the ongoing conflict in Ukraine, despite US President Donald Trump's calls for peace negotiations.

- **Congressional stock trading ban bill planned**: House Republican leaders announced plans to vote on a bill in the new year that would prohibit members of Congress from trading individual stocks, amid concerns about insider trading.

- **Ghislaine Maxwell petitions to vacate conviction**: Ghislaine Maxwell has filed a petition seeking to overturn her conviction related to Jeffrey Epstein, claiming new evidence undermines her case and alleging juror misconduct during her trial.

- **Kennedy Center to be renamed**: The John F. Kennedy Memorial Center for the Performing Arts will be renamed the "Trump-Kennedy Center," following a unanimous vote by its board, although this decision may face legal challenges.
