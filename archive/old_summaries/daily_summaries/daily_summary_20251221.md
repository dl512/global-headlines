- **US Seizes Oil Tanker**: The US has seized a second oil tanker off Venezuela's coast as part of a broader effort to combat illicit oil trafficking linked to Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro. Venezuela condemned the action as "theft and kidnapping," escalating tensions between the two nations.

- **Michaela Benthaus Goes to Space**: German engineer Michaela Benthaus made history as the first wheelchair user to travel to space aboard a Blue Origin flight. Her journey emphasizes the importance of accessibility in space exploration.
