As she seeks international support, her visit represents a critical moment for opposition movements in the country.

The situation has been further escalated by US President Donald Trump's administration, which has recently intensified its engagement towards Venezuela and other left-leaning governments in Latin America. Following the US seizure of an oil tanker off Venezuela's coast, the Venezuelan government condemned the action as an international crime, as outlined by [El Universal](http://www.eluniversal.com/politica/222136/venezuela-repudia-incautacion-de-tanquero-petrolero-en-el-caribe).
