## Tech News

- **JPMorgan Chase becomes the new issuer of the Apple Card**: JPMorgan Chase is set to replace Goldman Sachs as the issuer of the Apple Card, with the transition expected to take up to 24 months and projected to bring over $20 billion in card balances to Chase.

- **Artisan AI reinstated on LinkedIn after ban**: Artisan AI, a startup focused on AI agents for sales, has been reinstated on LinkedIn after addressing concerns over data scraping and compliance, highlighting the scrutiny AI companies face regarding platform policies.

- **OpenAI launches ChatGPT Health**: OpenAI has introduced ChatGPT Health, a platform for health-related discussions, which will integrate with personal health data from wellness apps, though it will not be used for diagnosing or treating conditions.
