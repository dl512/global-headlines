- **Space and Defense Stock Boom**: Satellite stocks surged over 200% in 2025, fueled by increased U.S. defense spending and interest in space exploration. The upcoming IPO of SpaceX is expected to further attract investor interest in this sector.

- **College Dropout Trend in Startups**: The trend of aspiring startup founders dropping out of college to pursue business opportunities is rising, particularly in the AI sector. While some venture capitalists value education, many believe that dropping out may enhance funding chances.

- **Pet Gadgets of 2025**: Innovative gadgets for pet owners, such as AI-powered cameras and automatic litter boxes, are gaining popularity, enhancing pet care through real-time monitoring and convenience.
