## Tech News

- **SoftBank Completes $40 Billion Investment in OpenAI**: SoftBank has finalized its $40 billion investment in OpenAI, increasing its stake to approximately 11%. This funding aims to bolster OpenAI's infrastructure and its collaboration with Oracle, reflecting the rising demand for AI applications.

- **Apple Under Pressure for AI-Enhanced Siri**: Apple is facing mounting pressure to launch a significantly upgraded AI version of Siri in 2026 after failing to deliver as promised in 2025. The company needs this breakthrough to regain investor confidence and boost sales amid fierce competition.

- **Waymo's Robotaxis Stuck During SF Blackout**: Waymo has explained the immobilization of its robotaxis during a recent blackout in San Francisco, highlighting the challenges autonomous vehicles face in unexpected situations and raising concerns about their reliability.

- **Elon Musk's Vision for Humanoid Robots**: Elon Musk envisions a future where humanoid robots are integrated across various sectors, but China is poised to lead in this technology, ramping up production and prioritizing robotics in its strategic plans.

- **Octopus Energy to Spin Off AI Unit Kraken**: UK-based Octopus Energy plans to spin off its AI unit, Kraken Technologies, valued at $8.65 billion following a $1 billion funding round. The spin-off aims to enhance its software offerings for utility companies.

## Climate and Investment News

- **Investors Discuss Future of Climate Tech**: Insights from 12 investors reveal that investment in climate tech remains stable despite political challenges, with a focus on energy resilience and zero-carbon power sources expected to grow in 2026.

- **Khanna Calls for Fraud Investigation Over Wealth Tax Proposal**: US Representative Ro Khanna is initiating a bipartisan investigation into potential fraud linked to a wealth tax proposal, emphasizing the need for public trust in government spending to support progressive initiatives.
