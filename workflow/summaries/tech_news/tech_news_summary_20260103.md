## Tech News

- **Tesla Annual Sales Decline**: Tesla's annual sales dropped by 9% in 2025, delivering 1.63 million vehicles, as it was overtaken by BYD, which sold 2.26 million EVs, marking a significant shift in the global electric vehicle market. The decline is attributed to increased competition and the removal of the U.S. federal tax credit.

- **BYD Becomes Top EV Seller**: Chinese automaker BYD has surpassed Tesla to become the world's leading seller of electric vehicles, achieving a 28% increase in sales to 2.26 million units in 2025, while Tesla's deliveries fell for the second consecutive year.

- **Nvidia's AI Investments Surge**: Nvidia has ramped up its investments in AI startups, participating in 67 venture capital deals in 2025, including a $10 billion commitment to Anthropic and a $6 billion round for Elon Musk's xAI, reflecting its strategy to expand the AI ecosystem.

- **Chip Stocks Rally**: Chip stocks, led by ASML, Intel, and Micron Technology, surged at the start of 2026 after a strong performance in 2025, driven by investments in AI and datacenter infrastructure, despite concerns about a potential AI bubble.

- **Baidu's Hong Kong IPO Plans**: Baidu plans to spin off its AI chip subsidiary, Kunlunxin, and pursue a Hong Kong IPO amid U.S.-China tech tensions, aiming to attract investors and highlight the subsidiary's standalone potential.

- **Bitcoin Hacker Released Early**: Ilya Lichtenstein, sentenced to five years for hacking the Bitfinex crypto exchange, was released early, crediting the First Step Act signed by US President Donald Trump, as he aims to positively impact cybersecurity.

- **Buffett Steps Down at Berkshire Hathaway**: Berkshire Hathaway shares dipped 1.4% as Warren Buffett officially stepped down as CEO, handing over leadership to Greg Abel, raising questions about the company's future performance amid a record cash reserve.

- **Logistics and Manufacturing Innovations**: TechCrunch's Startup Battlefield highlighted 16 innovative startups in logistics and manufacturing, showcasing advancements such as autonomous freight vehicles and AI platforms aimed at improving operational efficiency.

- **AI Boom in Hangzhou**: In Hangzhou, China's AI hub, startups are innovating in robotics and fortune-telling AI applications, reflecting a blend of advanced technology and grassroots experimentation, with companies preparing for public listings.
