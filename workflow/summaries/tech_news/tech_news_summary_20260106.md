## Tech News

- **Uber Unveils New Robotaxi**: Uber, in collaboration with Lucid Motors and Nuro, introduced a new robotaxi based on the Lucid Gravity SUV at CES 2026, with plans to launch commercial service in the San Francisco Bay Area later this year.

- **Nvidia's Robotics Ambitions**: Nvidia aims to become the leading platform for generalist robotics, unveiling a comprehensive ecosystem at CES 2026 that includes advanced robot foundation models and simulation tools.

- **Nvidia Launches Rubin Chip Architecture**: The new Rubin chip architecture from Nvidia, designed for AI's growing computational needs, promises performance improvements of up to 3.5 times for model training and 5 times for inference tasks.

- **Boston Dynamics Partners with Google DeepMind**: Boston Dynamics is enhancing its Atlas humanoid robot with AI models from Google DeepMind to improve human interaction capabilities, with production set for deployment in Hyundai's factory by 2028.

- **Nvidia Introduces Alpamayo AI Models**: Nvidia launched Alpamayo, a new family of open-source AI models aimed at improving the reasoning capabilities of autonomous vehicles, with expectations for deployment on U.S. roads in early 2026.

- **Lego's Screen-Free Smart Bricks**: At CES 2026, Lego introduced its Smart Play system, featuring interactive Smart Bricks that enhance play without screens, with initial Star Wars-themed sets launching in March.

- **Hacktivist Targets White Supremacist Websites**: A hacktivist deleted three white supremacist websites live at a hacker conference in Germany, exposing poor cybersecurity practices and personal data of users.

- **Offshore Wind Developers Sue US President Donald Trump**: Three offshore wind developers are suing the US President Donald Trump's administration after the Department of the Interior halted $25 billion in projects due to national security concerns.

- **Lucid Motors Doubles EV Production**: Lucid Motors reported a doubling of its electric vehicle output in 2025, producing 18,378 vehicles, recovering from earlier production struggles with its Gravity SUV.

- **Amazon Launches Alexa.com**: Amazon has launched a web-based version of its AI assistant, Alexa.com, aiming to enhance accessibility and integration into daily life, with a revamped mobile app experience.

- **Teradar Introduces Terahertz Vision Sensor**: Teradar unveiled its first terahertz-band vision sensor for cars at CES 2026, designed to enhance vehicle autonomy in various weather conditions, with shipping expected to start in 2028.

- **Liko.ai Secures Financing for AI Home Solutions**: Liko.ai completed its first financing round to develop an AI Home Center, focusing on enhancing user experience and privacy with innovative smart camera systems.

- **Embodied Intelligence Innovates Mountain Farming**: A startup focused on mountain farming has secured seed financing to develop intelligent farming machinery aimed at addressing labor shortages in hilly regions of China.

- **Yao Le Technology Completes Pre-A Financing**: Yao Le Technology raised nearly 100 million RMB in Pre-A financing, with plans to enhance core technology and expand market presence.

- **RayNeo Secures Over 1 Billion RMB Financing**: RayNeo completed a financing round led by China Mobile, aimed at advancing technology development and global market expansion, with plans to showcase new AR smart glasses.

- **Super Content Receives Angel Financing**: The podcast commercialization company Super Content secured 3 million yuan in angel financing, marking a significant step as it aims to capitalize on the podcast industry's potential growth.
