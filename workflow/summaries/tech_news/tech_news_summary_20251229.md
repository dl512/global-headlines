## Tech News

- **Sauron Appoints New CEO**: High-end home security startup Sauron has named Maxime Bouvat-Merlin, formerly of Sonos, as its new CEO, aiming to launch a military-grade security system for premium customers by late 2026.

- **OpenAI Seeks Head of Preparedness**: OpenAI is looking for a new Head of Preparedness to tackle emerging AI-related risks, with a compensation package of $555,000 plus equity, emphasizing the importance of security measures in AI development.

- **TikTok Spins Off US Operations**: TikTok has finalized a deal to separate its U.S. operations with an American investor group, aiming to address regulatory concerns and enhance its market position amid ongoing scrutiny over data privacy.

- **US Government Launches 'Tech Force'**: The U.S. government has initiated a new program called 'Tech Force' to recruit top talent in artificial intelligence, highlighting its commitment to enhancing technological capabilities.

- **President Donald Trump Blocks State AI Regulations**: US President Donald Trump has signed an executive order preventing states from enforcing their own regulations on artificial intelligence, aiming for a unified federal approach to governance in this sector.

- **Disney Invests in OpenAI**: Disney is investing $1 billion in OpenAI and will license its characters for the AI platform Sora, aiming to integrate its intellectual properties with advanced AI technologies.

- **Waymo Updates Fleet After Blackout**: Waymo is enhancing its fleet of autonomous vehicles following a recent blackout in San Francisco, aiming to improve navigation capabilities during outages.

- **India's Startup Funding Declines**: India's startup ecosystem raised nearly $11 billion in 2025, but the number of funding rounds dropped significantly, reflecting a shift in investor focus towards startups with stronger product-market fit.

- **Merriam-Webster's Word of the Year**: Merriam-Webster has chosen its 2025 word of the year to address concerns over the quality of AI-generated content, reflecting growing awareness of the challenges posed by artificial intelligence.

- **Nvidia Acquires AI Chip Startup Groq**: Nvidia is acquiring AI chip startup Groq for approximately $20 billion, raising concerns about competition in the AI sector and the implications for innovation.

- **Oracle Faces Share Decline**: Oracle's shares are on track for their worst quarterly performance since 2001 due to skepticism about its artificial intelligence build-out strategy.

- **Australian Social Media Ban for Teens**: Australia has enacted a ban on young teens from social media, prompting discussions about the potential for similar regulations in the U.S. to protect youth safety and mental health.
