## Tech News

- **China Mobile Chain Long Fund Supports Quantum Computing**: China's Zhongke Kuyan Technology has secured nearly 100 million yuan in strategic financing from the China Mobile Chain Long Fund to advance its atomic quantum computing system, aiming to lead in the domestic quantum technology sector.

- **OpenAI Acquires Health Records Startup Torch**: OpenAI has acquired the health records startup Torch for approximately $100 million, enhancing its ChatGPT Health service with technology that consolidates medical information from various sources.

- **Meta Launches AI Infrastructure Initiative**: US President Donald Trump announced that Meta is launching a new initiative called Meta Compute to significantly expand its AI infrastructure, aiming to build tens of gigawatts of energy capacity over the next decade.

- **Amazon Expands AI Wearable Market**: Amazon has acquired Bee, an AI wearable device designed for recording conversations, to enhance its consumer AI offerings beyond home devices like Alexa, raising privacy concerns about its use.

- **Apple Partners with Google for Siri Upgrade**: Apple has partnered with Google to utilize its Gemini AI technology for an upgraded version of Siri, expected to launch later this year, marking a significant move in enhancing Apple's AI capabilities.

- **Fintech Firm Betterment Confirms Data Breach**: Betterment has confirmed a data breach that allowed hackers to access personal information of some customers, although no login credentials were compromised, as the company investigates the incident.

- **Paramount Files Lawsuit Against Warner Bros.**: Paramount has filed a lawsuit against Warner Bros. Discovery, seeking greater financial disclosure regarding Netflix's acquisition of Warner Bros., amid concerns about the merger's implications for the media industry.

- **SK Hynix Invests in AI Memory Business**: SK Hynix announced a $13 billion investment to build a new advanced packaging plant in South Korea to meet the rising demand for high-bandwidth memory driven by AI applications.

- **Elizabeth Warren Raises Concerns Over Crypto in 401(k) Plans**: Senator Elizabeth Warren has expressed concerns about the risks associated with including cryptocurrencies in 401(k) retirement plans, warning that it could lead to significant financial losses for American workers.

- **Malaysia and Indonesia Block Elon Musk's Grok**: Both countries have blocked access to Elon Musk's AI chatbot Grok due to concerns over its generation of nonconsensual sexual content, following repeated failures by xAI to address these risks.

- **Harmattan AI Becomes Defense Unicorn**: French defense tech company Harmattan AI has raised $200 million in a Series B funding round, achieving a valuation of $1.4 billion and focusing on enhancing its software for defense applications.

- **Developers Earn $550 Billion on Apple's App Store**: Since its launch in 2008, developers have earned $550 billion from Apple's App Store, reflecting significant growth and ongoing scrutiny over Apple's commission practices.

- **Trump's EPA Plans to Ignore Health Effects of Air Pollution**: The Trump administration's EPA plans to disregard health impacts of air pollution in regulatory practices, a significant shift welcomed by the U.S. Chamber of Commerce amid increasing reliance on polluting energy sources.
