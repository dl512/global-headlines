## Tech News

- **CES 2026 Highlights**: Major tech companies showcased innovations at CES 2026, with Nvidia unveiling its Rubin architecture and AI models for autonomous vehicles, while AMD introduced new processors aimed at enhancing AI capabilities. Razer shifted focus to AI with its Project AVA and smart glasses alternative, and Lego debuted a Smart Play System.

- **Mobileye Acquires Mentee Robotics**: Mobileye has acquired humanoid robot startup Mentee Robotics for $900 million, marking a strategic move into robotics as part of its vision for "Mobileye 3.0," integrating humanoid robotics with automotive AI.

- **LMArena Valuation Surge**: LMArena, a startup from UC Berkeley, has reached a $1.7 billion valuation just four months post-launch, following a $150 million Series A funding round, driven by its crowdsourced AI model performance leaderboards.

- **Spyware Maker's Guilty Plea**: Bryan Fleming, founder of spyware company pcTattletale, pleaded guilty to federal charges of hacking and advertising surveillance software, marking a significant prosecution in the stalkerware industry.

- **Threads Develops In-Message Games**: Threads is working on in-message games, including a basketball game, to enhance user engagement and compete with platforms like X and Bluesky, despite currently trailing in U.S. usage.

- **Amazon's AI Tool Controversy**: Amazon's new AI shopping tool, "Shop Direct," has faced backlash from online retailers for listing their products without consent, leading to complaints about incorrect listings and orders.

- **Nvidia's Demand in China**: Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang reported "very high" demand for H200 AI chips in China, highlighting a potential $50 billion annual opportunity following U.S. government approval for exports.

- **Meta's Legal Leadership Change**: Meta appointed Curtis Joseph Mahoney as its new chief legal officer amid ongoing child-safety lawsuits, replacing Jennifer Newstead.

- **Elon Musk's xAI Funding Success**: xAI raised $20 billion in a funding round, exceeding its target, with notable investors including Nvidia and Cisco, valuing the AI startup at approximately $230 billion.

- **AMD's Hiring Focus**: AMD CEO Lisa Su stated the company is prioritizing candidates who embrace AI in its hiring process, reflecting the changing job market due to AI technologies.

- **Meta Delays Ray-Ban Display Rollout**: Meta has postponed the global rollout of its Ray-Ban Display AI glasses due to inventory limits and high U.S. demand, focusing on fulfilling existing orders.

- **NinjaOne Revenue Milestone**: Software startup NinjaOne has surpassed $500 million in annual recurring revenue, achieving significant growth and planning to launch new products.

- **Semiconductor Stock Rally**: Memory chip giants Micron, SK Hynix, and Samsung have led a rally in semiconductor stocks, driven by rising demand for memory chips essential for AI applications, with prices expected to increase significantly.

- **Misinformation Following Maduro's Removal**: Misleading AI-generated videos celebrating the removal of Venezuelan leader Nicolas Maduro have gone viral, raising concerns about misinformation on social media.

- **DeepSeek's Market Impact Decline**: After initially causing a market upheaval with its reasoning model, DeepSeek has failed to replicate the same impact with subsequent releases, as U.S. tech firms continue to dominate.

- **Starlink Future SATELLAI Funding**: Starlink Future SATELLAI completed a Series A funding round to advance AI technology in pet health and safety, targeting the global pet market.

- **Clipto.AI Funding Success**: AI company Clipto.AI completed a Pre-A++ funding round, achieving a valuation of over $250 million, focusing on multimodal AI models.

- **Qiangnao Technology Funding**: Brain-computer interface startup Qiangnao Technology secured 2 billion yuan in funding, marking significant investment in the sector.

- **OBOO Technology Funding**: OBOO Technology raised 18 million yuan in Pre-A funding to enhance its AI and industrial display strategy.

- **Han'ao Intelligent Funding**: Han'ao Intelligent completed an angel round funding to promote electrolytic milling technology for high-precision manufacturing.

- **Liko.ai Funding Round**: Liko.ai completed its first funding round to develop an AI Home Center and intelligent home solutions.

- **Embodied Intelligence Funding**: A startup focused on mountain farming secured seed funding to develop technology addressing labor shortages in hilly regions of China.
