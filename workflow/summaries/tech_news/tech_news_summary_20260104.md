## Tech News

- **California Residents Gain Data Control**: A new tool, the Delete Requests and Opt-Out Platform (DROP), allows California residents to request the deletion of their personal data from over 500 data brokers, enhancing consumer privacy and simplifying the process.

- **Stock Market Performance Review**: The stock market has shown mixed results at the start of 2026, with slight declines in major indices despite a strong performance in 2025. Key stock movements include GE Vernova and Corning outperforming the market, while Salesforce and Nike lagged.

- **Anthropic's AI Strategy**: Daniela Amodei, co-founder of Anthropic, advocates for a strategy of "doing more with less" in the AI sector, emphasizing efficiency over scale in contrast to competitors like OpenAI, which has heavily invested in infrastructure.

- **Weiyuan Synthesis Secures Funding**: Weiyuan Synthesis has raised nearly 300 million Yuan to develop bio-based products using methanol, aiming to improve China's self-sufficiency in amino acids and address raw material challenges in the industry.
