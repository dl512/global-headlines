## Tech News

- **AI Industry Leadership and Talent Turnover**: Rapid employee turnover continues at AI labs, notably Mira Murati’s Thinking Machines Lab, with key executives leaving for OpenAI amid fierce competition for top talent. OpenAI and Anthropic are actively poaching senior safety researchers and engineers, highlighting industry instability.

- **US-China Semiconductor and AI Developments**: Taiwan announced a $250 billion investment in US semiconductor manufacturing, aiming to reduce reliance on foreign supply chains and bolster US industry. Meanwhile, the US imposed a 25% tariff on Nvidia’s H200 AI chips headed to China, allowing Nvidia to sell to vetted Chinese customers despite ongoing export restrictions.

- **US Regulatory and Legal Actions**: The FTC settled its data-sharing order with General Motors, requiring stricter consumer data privacy measures. US senators demanded platforms like X, Meta, and Alphabet provide detailed policies on combating sexualized deepfakes, amid rising concerns over AI-generated explicit content.

- **Chinese Tech and Investment Growth**: China’s AI chip IPO boom continues, with firms like Moore Threads raising over $1.1 billion, though Huawei remains privately dominant. Chinese startups like EtherealX, developing reusable launch vehicles, have seen valuation jumps, and local semiconductor materials companies are securing hundreds of millions of yuan in funding to advance manufacturing.

- **US and China AI Collaboration and Competition**: Google DeepMind’s CEO states China is only “a matter of months” behind US AI capabilities, signaling rapid progress. Wikimedia’s new AI partnerships with Amazon, Meta, and Microsoft aim to integrate Wikipedia data into AI models, supporting knowledge accuracy amid AI content reuse.

- **Major Corporate and Investment Moves**: Parloa, a Berlin-based AI customer service startup, tripled its valuation to $3 billion after an $350 million funding round. WeLab, a fintech firm, raised $220 million in Series D funding to expand in Southeast Asia. Cloudflare acquired UK AI data marketplace Human Native to support transparent data access for AI development.

- **Global Geopolitical and Market Impacts**: International tensions involving Greenland and Iran have driven investors toward gold and silver as safe havens. US President Donald Trump’s administration approved Nvidia’s H200 chip sales to China with a 25% surcharge, balancing market access with national security concerns.

- **Emerging Technologies and Scientific Discoveries**: NASA announced the discovery of a rock on Mars showing the “clearest sign” of ancient life, marking a breakthrough in planetary science. China’s Super Magnetic Energy secured funding to develop controlled nuclear fusion technology, aiming to advance clean energy solutions.

- **Other Notable Developments**: Australian authorities’ social media ban for under-16s faces mixed results, with teens bypassing restrictions via VPNs. India’s Emversity doubled its valuation to $120 million, focusing on training roles that AI cannot replace, while Indian startup EtherealX prepares for commercial launches of reusable rockets by 2028.
