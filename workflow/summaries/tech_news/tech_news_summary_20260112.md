## Tech News

- **Google Co-Founders May Be Leaving California**: Sergey Brin and Larry Page are reportedly reducing their presence in California by terminating or relocating multiple limited liability companies to Nevada, potentially to avoid a new 5% tax on billionaires if a ballot measure passes later this year.

- **Huaxin Micro-Nano Completes Series A Financing**: Huaxin Micro-Nano has successfully completed its Series A financing, with undisclosed amounts raised from institutions like Anhui High-tech Investment and China National New Holding, positioning it as a leading 8-inch MEMS wafer foundry in China.

- **ByteDance, Meituan, and Alibaba Bet Together as Zivariable Completes 1 Billion Yuan A++ Round Financing**: Zivariable has raised 1 billion yuan in an A++ round of financing, backed by major firms including ByteDance, Meituan, and Alibaba, marking a significant investment from Shenzhen Capital Group's AI fund since its inception.
