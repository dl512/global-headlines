## Tech News

- **Google moonshot spinout SandboxAQ claims an ex-exec is attempting ‘extortion’**: SandboxAQ is facing a wrongful termination lawsuit from former Chief of Staff Robert Bender, who alleges misconduct by CEO Jack Hidary. The company has denied the allegations, calling Bender a "serial liar" and suggesting the lawsuit is an extortion attempt, raising concerns about corporate governance in Silicon Valley.

- **The venture firm that ate Silicon Valley just raised another $15 billion**: Andreessen Horowitz has raised over $15 billion in new funding, increasing its total assets under management to more than $90 billion. The firm plans to invest in various sectors, including AI and U.S. defense priorities, solidifying its position as one of the largest venture firms globally.
