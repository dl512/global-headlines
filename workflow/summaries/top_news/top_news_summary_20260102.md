## Most Important Stories

- **Around 40 dead in Swiss ski resort bar fire**: A fire at a bar in Crans-Montana, Switzerland, during New Year's celebrations has resulted in approximately 40 fatalities and over 115 injuries, with many victims suffering severe burns. The cause of the fire is under investigation, but initial reports suggest it may have started from birthday candles igniting the wooden ceiling.

- **Iran protests turn deadly**: Clashes between protesters and security forces in Iran have led to at least six deaths as demonstrations against rising living costs continue. The unrest, which has spread across multiple cities, has prompted authorities to implement tight security measures, including school closures.

- **China's BYD set to overtake Tesla**: Chinese electric vehicle manufacturer BYD is on track to surpass Tesla as the world's top EV seller, with sales increasing by nearly 28% last year. This growth highlights BYD's competitive pricing strategy and the challenges Tesla faces amid rising competition.

- **US sanctions on Iranian and Venezuelan groups**: The US has imposed sanctions on ten individuals and entities linked to the weapons trade in Iran and Venezuela, including a company involved in Iranian drone sales. US President Donald Trump has warned of potential military action against Iran if it attempts to rebuild its weapons programs
