## Most Important Stories

- **Trump's Greenland Ambitions**: US President Donald Trump has reiterated his desire for the U.S. to acquire Greenland, claiming it is vital for national security to prevent Russian and Chinese influence. Both Denmark and Greenland have firmly rejected the proposal, stating it is not for sale.

This move follows a recent U.S. military operation that captured President Nicolás Maduro, with President Donald Trump announcing the cancellation of further military attacks on the country.

This decision follows the capture of Maduro and reflects growing concerns over U.S. military involvement in the region.

- **Judge Blocks Trump's Aid Freeze**: A U.S. judge has temporarily blocked President Donald Trump's administration from freezing over $10 billion in federal aid for child care and family assistance to five states, citing concerns about fraud and the legality of the freeze.

- **Lebanon's Hezbollah Disarmament Plan**: Lebanon announced the completion of the first phase of its plan to disarm Hezbollah, although Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu criticized the progress as "far from sufficient." This development highlights ongoing regional tensions and the challenges of achieving lasting peace.

- **Russia's Stance on Ukraine**: Russia has declared that any foreign military forces in Ukraine would be considered "legitimate targets," following troop pledges from the UK and France. This statement escalates tensions in the ongoing conflict and signals potential repercussions for foreign involvement.

- **Cybercrime Kingpin Extradited**: Chen Zhi, an alleged cybercrime kingpin, has been arrested and extradited from Cambodia to China, highlighting international efforts to combat cybercrime and enhance cooperation between nations in addressing criminal activities.
