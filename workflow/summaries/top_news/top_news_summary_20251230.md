As US President Donald Trump advocates for a peace deal, Russia's strategy appears focused on territorial gains to strengthen its negotiating position.

- **Thailand and Cambodia Sign Ceasefire Agreement**: Thailand and Cambodia have signed a ceasefire to halt intense border clashes that have escalated recently, aiming to restore peace and stability in the disputed areas along their shared border.
