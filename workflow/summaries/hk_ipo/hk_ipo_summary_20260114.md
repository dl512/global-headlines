## HK IPO News Summary

- **LONGCHEER (09611.HK)**: LONGCHEER's IPO commenced on January 14, 2026, with an entry fee of approximately HKD3,131.3. The company is offering 52.259 million H-shares globally, with around 10% allocated for the Hong Kong public offering at an offer price of HKD31 per share. (Sources: [AAStocks](https://www.aastocks.com/en/stocks/news/aafn-con/NOW.1495922/ipo-news/AAFN))

- **EASTROC BEVERAGE (605499.SH), MUYUAN (002714.SZ)**: Both EASTROC BEVERAGE and MUYUAN are set to launch pre-IPO roadshows in Hong Kong as early as January 14, 2026. They aim to raise USD1 billion and USD1.5 billion respectively. (Sources: [AAStocks](https://www.aastocks.com/en/stocks/news/aafn-con/NOW.1495921/ipo-news/AAFN))
