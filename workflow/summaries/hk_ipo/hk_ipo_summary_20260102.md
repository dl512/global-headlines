## HK IPO News Summary

- **BIREN TECH (06082.HK)**: On February 1, 2026, BIREN TECH opened at $35.7, up 82.1% from its listing price of $19.6, with 47.67 million shares traded at market open. The stock had closed at $35.22 on the gray market the previous day, reflecting a 79.7% increase from the listing price. (Sources: [AAStocks](https://www.aastocks.com/en/stocks/news/aafn-con/NOW.1493413/ipo-news/AAFN))

- **BIDU-SW (09888.HK)**: On February 1, 2026, BIDU-SW announced that Kunlunxin has confidentially submitted a listing application to the Hong Kong Stock Exchange, seeking permission to list its shares on the Main Board. (Sources: [AAStocks](https://www.aastocks.com/en/stocks/news/aafn-con/NOW.1493357/ipo-news/AAFN))

- **TENCENT (00700.HK)**: On February 1, 2026, it was reported that Enflame, a leading AI chip company and one of the "Four Dragons of Domestic GPU," has completed its IPO guidance work, moving forward in its listing process. TENCENT is noted as the largest shareholder of Enflame. (Sources: [AAStocks](https://www.aastocks.com/en/stocks/news/aafn-con/NOW.1493355/ipo-news/AAFN))

- **ROBOROCK (00388.HK)**: On February 1, 2026, Beijing Roborock Technology submitted its IPO application to the HKEX, with JPMorgan and CITIC Securities as joint sponsors. The company focuses on the smart robot vacuum sector, generating approximately 90% of its revenue from smart robot vacuum cleaner sales. (Sources: [AAStocks](https://www.aastocks.com/en/stocks/news/aafn-con/NOW.1493372/ipo-news/AAFN))
