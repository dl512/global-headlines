## HK IPO News Summary

- **Standard Robots**: On January 5, 2026, Standard Robots applied for a listing on the Hong Kong main board, with joint sponsors CITIC Securities and Guotai Haitong. The company's revenue for the first nine months of 2025 reached RMB188 million, marking a 19.66% year-on-year increase, while it reported a loss attributable to shareholders of RMB163 million. (Sources: [AAStocks](https://www.aastocks.com/en/stocks/news/aafn-con/NOW.1493706/ipo-news/AAFN))
