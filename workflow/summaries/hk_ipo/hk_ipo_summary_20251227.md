## HK IPO News Summary

- **BIREN TECH (06082.HK)**: On December 22, BIREN TECH commenced its IPO in Hong Kong with an entry fee of approximately $3,960, aiming to become the first GPU stock listed in Hong Kong. The company plans to offer 248 million H shares globally, with 5% allocated for the Hong Kong public offering. (Sources: [AAStocks](https://www.aastocks.com/en/stocks/news/aafn-con/NOW.1491716/ipo-news/AAFN))

- **MiniMax**: On December 23, the CSRC approved MiniMax Group Inc.'s plan for overseas issuance and listing, allowing the company to issue up to 33.5772 million shares. Additionally, on December 22, MiniMax cleared the HKEX listing hearing, specializing in general AI technology. (Sources: [AAStocks](https://www.aastocks.com/en/stocks/news/aafn-con/NOW.1491987/ipo-news/AAFN), [AAStocks](https://www.aastocks.com/en/stocks/news/aafn-con/NOW.1491698/ipo-news/AAFN))
