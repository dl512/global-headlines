## HK IPO News Summary

- **GIGADEVICE (03986.HK)**: The fabless chipmaker announced its Hong Kong IPO today, planning to offer 28.9158 million H-shares globally, with 10% for the Hong Kong public and 90% for international placement. (Sources: [AAStocks](https://www.aastocks.com/en/stocks/news/aafn-con/NOW.1493135/ipo-news/AAFN))
  
- **MINIMAX-WP (00100.HK)**: The AI large model firm commenced its IPO today, aiming to offer over 25.3892 million shares globally, with 5% allocated for the Hong Kong public offering, and has secured 14 cornerstone investors including BABA-W. (Sources: [AAStocks](https://www.aastocks.com/en/stocks/news/aafn-con/NOW.1493118/ipo-news/AAFN))
