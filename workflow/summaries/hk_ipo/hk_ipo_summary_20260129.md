- **Kuxin Microelectronics**: Submitted HKEX Main Board listing application on January 29, focusing on AI SoC products. (Sources: [AAStocks](https://www.aastocks.com/en/stocks/news/aafn-con/NOW.1499549/ipo-news/AAFN))
