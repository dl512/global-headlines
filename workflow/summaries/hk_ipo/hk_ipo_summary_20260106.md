## HK IPO News Summary

- **KNOWLEDGE ATLAS (02513.HK)**: The public offering for KNOWLEDGE ATLAS was oversubscribed approximately 1,164 times, attracting over 200,000 applications, ahead of its listing on June 8. (Sources: [AAStocks](https://www.aastocks.com/en/stocks/news/aafn-con/NOW.1493988/ipo-news/AAFN))
  
- **LEAD INTELLIGENT (300450.SZ)**: LEAD INTELLIGENT plans to issue around 200 million H shares, as notified by the China Securities Regulatory Commission (CSRC). (Sources: [AAStocks](https://www.aastocks.com/en/stocks/news/aafn-con/NOW.1493955/ipo-news/AAFN))

- **Roborock (688169.SH)**: Roborock has filed with the CSRC to issue up to 33.108 million overseas listed ordinary shares, intending to list on the HKEX (00388.HK). (Sources: [AAStocks](https://www.aastocks.com/en/stocks/news/aafn-con/NOW.1493963/ipo-news/AAFN))
