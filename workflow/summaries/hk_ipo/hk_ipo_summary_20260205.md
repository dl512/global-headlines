## HK IPO News Summary

- WOER (09981.HK) [launched its IPO](https://www.aastocks.com/en/stocks/news/aafn-con/NOW.1501249/ipo-news/AAFN) on February 5, aiming to raise up to HKD2.81 billion by offering nearly 140 million H-shares at a maximum price of HKD20.09, with an entry fee of HKD4,058.53.
- HAIZHI TECH GP (02706.HK) [began its IPO](https://www.aastocks.com/en/stocks/news/aafn-con/NOW.1501234/ipo-news/AAFN) on February 5, targeting up to HKD785 million by issuing 28.03 million H-shares at HKD25.6-28 per share, with a one-lot entry fee of approximately HKD5,657.
