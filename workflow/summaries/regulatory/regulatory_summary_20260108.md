## Regulatory Announcements

- **Seyond Holdings Launches Warrant Exercise Period**: Seyond Holdings Ltd. has announced the commencement of the exercise period for its Successor Company Listed Warrants, effective January 9, 2026, through December 10, 2030. Each warrant can be exercised at HK$11.50 per share, with a maximum conversion ratio of 0.425 shares per warrant on a cashless basis. The company may redeem unexercised warrants if the share price exceeds HK$20.00 for 20 trading days within a 30-day period. A total of 50,050,000 Successor Company Listed Warrants and 40,000,000 Promoter Warrants are outstanding, allowing for a maximum issuance of 38,271,250 shares upon full exercise. (Source: [Seyond Holdings](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0108/2026010800766.pdf))
