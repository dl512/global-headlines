## Regulatory Announcements

- **Baidu Plans Spin-Off of Kunlunxin**: Baidu, Inc. has proposed a spin-off and separate listing of its subsidiary, Kunlunxin, on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange, with a confidential listing application submitted on January 1, 2026. The spin-off will involve a Global Offering, but Baidu has waived the requirement for existing shareholders to have assured entitlement to shares. Investors are advised to exercise caution as the spin-off is not guaranteed. (Source: [Baidu](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0102/2026010200013.pdf))

- **XPeng Reports Record EV Deliveries**: XPeng Inc. announced a total of 37,508 Smart EV deliveries in December 2025, marking a 2% year-over-year increase, and total annual deliveries of 429,445 units, up 126%. The company also expanded its international presence, delivering 45,008 vehicles overseas, and added over 1,100 new charging stations in 2025. Investors should remain cautious due to inherent risks in forward-looking statements. (Source: [XPeng](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0101/2026010100053.pdf))

- **NIO Achieves Record Deliveries in 2025**: NIO Inc. reported record-high deliveries with 48,135 vehicles delivered in December 2025, a 54.6% increase year-over-year. Total deliveries for the year reached 326,028, up 46.9%. The company highlighted the success of its All-New ES8 SUV and emphasized the risks associated with forward-looking statements. (Source: [NIO](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0101/2026010100039.pdf))

- **Li Auto Surpasses 1.5 Million Deliveries**: Li Auto Inc. delivered 44,246 vehicles in December 2025, bringing total deliveries for the quarter to 109,194 and cumulative deliveries to 1,540,215. The company also expanded its market presence to Central Asia and launched new AI glasses. Investors are cautioned about potential risks in forward-looking statements. (Source: [Li Auto](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0101/2026010100021.pdf))
