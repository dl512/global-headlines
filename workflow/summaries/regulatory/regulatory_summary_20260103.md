## Regulatory Announcements

- **Baidu Plans Spin-Off of AI Chip Subsidiary Kunlunxin**: Baidu, Inc. has announced its intention to spin off its subsidiary Kunlunxin, a provider of AI computing chips, and seek a separate listing on the Main Board of the Hong Kong Stock Exchange. A confidential listing application was submitted on January 1, 2026. The spin-off will involve a public offering in Hong Kong and a placement to institutional investors, although specific details are still pending. Baidu has received a waiver from the requirement to provide existing shareholders with assured entitlement to Kunlunxin shares, citing excessive costs and regulatory burdens. Investors are advised to exercise caution as there is no guarantee that the spin-off will proceed. (Source: [Baidu](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0102/2026010200013.pdf))
