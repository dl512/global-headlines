## Hong Kong News

- **Government to Issue Dog-Friendly Restaurant Licenses**: Hong Kong will issue 500-1,000 licenses this year allowing dogs in select restaurants, excluding hotpot and barbecue venues, with strict hygiene rules and a lottery if oversubscribed.
- **Surge in Data Breaches and Privacy Complaints**: The Privacy Commissioner reported a 20-21% rise in data breaches in 2025, mainly affecting schools and NGOs, with hacking cases and privacy complaints also increasing.
- **Electric Vehicle ‘One-for-One’ Scheme Spurs Market Rush**: The government’s EV exchange program expiring March 31 has triggered a surge in car purchases and quota trading, with speculation about possible policy tightening and tax changes.
- **Ma Tau Wai and Western District Estate Relocation Allowances**: The Housing Authority clarified that moving allowances for estate redevelopment are partial assistance, not full coverage, with extra support available for those in need.
- **Pan Cheuk-hung Faces Bankruptcy Deadline**: Artist Pan Cheuk-hung must repay HKD 690,000 by next Thursday or face a bankruptcy order, as he disputes the debt and claims his accounts are frozen.
- **Cold Snap Expected This Weekend**: The Observatory forecasts temperatures dropping to 13°C on Sunday, an 8°C fall in one day, due to a cold front and northeast monsoon.
- **Youth Sentenced to Reformative Institution for Scam Involvement**: An 18-year-old previously convicted of money laundering was sent to a reformative school after collecting scam proceeds from an elderly victim.
- **Mainland Man Jailed for Attempted Rape and Assault**: A 27-year-old mainland Chinese man received 5.5 years in prison for attempted rape and violent assault on a neighbor, citing psychological pressure and schizophrenia.
- **Taxi Driver Ticketed for ‘Lying Flat’ Driving**: A 51-year-old taxi driver was fined for careless driving after being filmed reclining while his vehicle moved in Kwai Chung.
- **Security Company Violates Privacy Ordinance**: A security firm was warned for sharing an employee’s termination notice in a workgroup chat, breaching privacy laws.
- **Hong Kong to Amend Teacher Training Requirements**: The Education Bureau plans to revise regulations requiring teachers to complete 150 hours of professional training every three years, with concerns about substitute teachers’ ability to comply.
- **Public Housing Turnover Sparks Online Excitement**: Increased online posts about returning public housing units have generated optimism among netizens about housing opportunities.
- **Gold Price Volatility Amid Global Uncertainty**: Gold prices have fluctuated due to market speculation and geopolitical tensions, but analysts remain optimistic about long-term appreciation and central bank demand.

- **Rise in Youth Involvement in Scams**: Courts highlight ongoing efforts to address youth participation in scams and criminal activities, with reformative sentences preferred for young offenders.
