## Global Financial News

- **Asia-Pacific Markets Decline**: Asia-Pacific markets fell on the last trading day of the year, with Hong Kong's Hang Seng Index down 0.42% and Australia's S&P/ASX 200 decreasing by 0.17%. Despite these losses, the MSCI All Country World Index reached a record high earlier in the week, reflecting a 21% annual increase.

- **China's Manufacturing Activity Expands**: China's manufacturing sector showed signs of recovery in December, with the official purchasing managers index rising to 50.1, surpassing expectations. This marks the first expansion since March, indicating stabilizing demand, although smaller firms still face challenges.

- **MiniMax IPO Launch**: MiniMax, a leading AI large model company, has launched its initial public offering (IPO) with cornerstone investments from Alibaba and Abu Dhabi, setting a minimum subscription amount of HKD 3,333.28.

- **MMG's Share Increase**: MMG's shares rose following the approval of an expansion project for the Khoemacau copper mine in Botswana, which is expected to enhance production capacity and improve financial performance.

- **Conant Optics Stock Rise**: Conant Optics saw an increase in stock value as smart glasses were included in a national subsidy program for the first time, potentially unlocking growth opportunities across the supply chain.

- **Upcoming IPOs in Semiconductor Sector**: GigaDevice, an integrated circuit design company, announced its IPO set for January 13, 2026, with a subscription amount of HKD 16,363.38. Meanwhile, Omnivision Group, a fabless semiconductor design company, is preparing for its IPO on January 12, 2026, with a lower minimum subscription of HKD 10,585.69.

- **Ribo Biotech-B IPO Announcement**: Ribo Biotech-B, a leader in small nucleic acid drugs and technology, has launched its IPO scheduled for January 9, 2026, with a minimum subscription of HKD 11,710.93, highlighting growing interest in biotechnology.

- **Market Trends in Precious Metals**: A report discusses the anticipated shift in investment trends towards precious metals as energy markets decline, indicating a significant reallocation of resources and investor focus.

- **U.S. Stocks Close with Declines**: U.S. stocks closed lower for the third consecutive session, with major indexes declining amid muted trading sentiment. Spot silver surged over 5%, while Chinese electric vehicle stocks, including XPeng, rallied.

- **CME Group Raises Margin Requirements**: The CME Group has increased margin requirements for precious metal futures due to heightened market volatility, affecting contracts for gold, silver, platinum, and palladium. This decision follows significant price fluctuations in precious metals, particularly silver.
