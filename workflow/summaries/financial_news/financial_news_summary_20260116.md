## Global Financial News

- **Taiwan Semiconductor's Record Earnings**: Taiwan Semiconductor reported a strong Q4 2025 with revenue of $33.73 billion, up 20.5%, and net profit of $16.3 billion, up 41%, driven by robust demand for AI and HPC chips. The company plans a capital expenditure of $52-56 billion in 2026, focusing on advanced process technologies, signaling a positive industry outlook despite capacity constraints.

- **US-China Trade and Semiconductor Sector**: Following a trade deal between the US and Taiwan, Taiwanese chip stocks, especially TSMC, surged, with Taiwan's market outperforming others in the region. European stocks also rallied on the back of TSMC's earnings, with ASML and ASM International reaching record highs amid investor optimism in the semiconductor sector.

- **US Market and Federal Reserve Outlook**: The US stock market closed higher, buoyed by strong earnings from Morgan Stanley and Goldman Sachs, and positive guidance from Taiwan Semiconductor. Market expectations are shifting towards a pause in Federal Reserve rate cuts, with some analysts even predicting no cuts in 2026, amid resilient US labor data and internal Fed debates. Federal Reserve Chair Powell has gained popularity for his leadership, with some officials signaling a potential pause in rate reductions despite pressure from President Donald Trump.

- **US Political and Economic Developments**: President Donald Trump has unveiled a healthcare plan aimed at reducing drug prices and increasing transparency. Meanwhile, he has made 189 bond purchases totaling over $51 million since January 2025, raising concerns about conflicts of interest. The US budget deficit has decreased significantly by $109 billion in the first quarter, mainly due to increased tariff revenues.

- **Hong Kong Market and Financing**: Hong Kong-listed companies engaged in nearly HKD 25 billion in secondary offerings in early 2026, with notable deals from SF Holding, J&T Express, and GF Securities. Kuaishou plans to raise about USD 20 billion via bonds, reflecting strong demand for equity and debt financing in the region.

- **US Political and Market Sentiment**: President Donald Trump’s recent financial disclosures reveal investments in bonds affected by his policies, fueling concerns about conflicts of interest. Additionally, market sentiment remains cautious as debates over Federal Reserve policy continue, with some officials emphasizing the need for a restrictive stance to control inflation.

- **Geopolitical and Regional Tensions**: Asian markets, including Japan and South Korea, reached record highs amid expectations of snap elections and regional optimism. However, geopolitical tensions persist, with Iran protests, US and Greenland talks over Arctic interests, and concerns over Venezuela's oil exports influencing global markets. European defense stocks rallied amid rising geopolitical tensions, including US threats to seize Greenland.

- **Commodities and Safe-Haven Assets**: Gold and silver prices hit near-record highs, with gold surpassing $4,600 per ounce, driven by geopolitical tensions, US rate cut bets, and global uncertainties. Oil prices climbed for the fifth consecutive day, amid fears of Iranian supply disruptions and regional tensions, despite a surprise build in US stockpiles.

- **China's Economic and Corporate Developments**: Chinese companies like Luoyang Molybdenum and Jiangxi Copper hit record highs, with Luoyang projecting a 53.71% increase in net profit for 2025. Jiangxi Copper's subsidiary signed an exploration agreement with First Quantum Kazakhstan, signaling active investment in mining. CGN Mining produced over 700 tons of uranium in Q4, with prices expected to rise due to supply tightness.

- **Technology and Innovation**: US and Chinese tech firms saw notable developments. Contex Optical's stock hit a record high as Meta plans to double smart glasses production, while Hesai's stock rose on expectations of full commercial deployment of autonomous vehicles and expanding LiDAR shipments. Ford is negotiating with BYD to purchase batteries, supporting its shift towards hybrid models.

- **Defense and Infrastructure**: European defense stocks rallied amid rising geopolitical tensions, with US calls for increased military spending and threats to seize Greenland. In China, the State Grid announced a 4 trillion yuan investment plan to expand power infrastructure, supporting high-quality development in energy and AI data centers.

- **Notable Personal and Corporate Stories**: Gene Yu, a former US Army Special Forces officer, founded cybersecurity startup Blackpanda, which raised over $21 million. Kim Greene, a former policy advisor, now sells luxury protection dogs to the wealthy, with sales reaching nearly $3 million in 2024.

- **Market and Policy Outlook**: Analysts note a shift in expectations towards a potential pause or delay in US rate cuts, influenced by resilient economic data and internal Fed debates. Meanwhile, discussions about internal disagreements within the Fed and political influences continue to shape monetary policy outlooks.
