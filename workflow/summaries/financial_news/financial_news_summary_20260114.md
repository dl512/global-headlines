## Global Financial News

- **Japanese stocks hit record high**: Japanese stocks reached new heights as expectations for a snap election by Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi rise, with the Nikkei 225 index climbing 1.51% and the yen weakening past 159 against the dollar.

- **China's trade surplus surges**: China's annual trade surplus hit a record $1.2 trillion in 2025, driven by a 6.6% increase in December exports, surpassing expectations, while imports rose by 5.7%, indicating strong trade performance amid U.S. tensions.

- **U.S. eases chip export controls**: The United States has relaxed export controls on NVIDIA's H200 chips to China, allowing sales under new regulatory conditions, with oversight from the U.S. Department of Commerce.

- **Spot silver surpasses $90**: Spot silver has reached a historic milestone, surpassing the $90 mark for the first time, reflecting a nearly 26% increase since the beginning of the year, indicating a bullish trend in the silver market.

- **Price hikes in wafer foundry services**: There has been a collective price increase of up to 20% in 8-inch wafer foundry services, reflecting ongoing trends in the semiconductor industry that may impact production costs.

- **Hua Hong Semiconductor shares rise**: Hua Hong Semiconductor's share price increased, supported by Goldman Sachs' observation of high capacity utilization enhancing product mix optimization and average selling price increases.

- **President Donald Trump calls for rate cuts**: President Donald Trump stated that inflation is low enough for the Federal Reserve to significantly cut interest rates, amid ongoing discussions about monetary policy's impact on the economy.

- **Significant trial for the Federal Reserve**: An upcoming trial is deemed even more critical for the Federal Reserve's independence than the recent subpoena received by Powell, with substantial implications expected for the market.

- **Zhipu's stock surges with Huawei partnership**: Zhipu's stock price surged following its partnership with Huawei to open-source the first domestically produced chip-trained multimodal SOTA model, showcasing advancements in technology.
