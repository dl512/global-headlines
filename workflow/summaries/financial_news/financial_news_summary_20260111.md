## Global Financial News

- **Starlink Expands Again**: The FCC has approved SpaceX's plan to deploy 7,500 second-generation Starlink satellites, significantly expanding its satellite internet service to enhance global coverage and service quality.

- **US President Donald Trump Considers Military Strikes on Iran**: US President Donald Trump is reviewing a potential new round of military strikes against Iran, referred to as "Midnight Hammer," which could escalate regional tensions, though details on timing or scale remain unclear.

- **Storage Market Shifts to Seller's Cycle**: SanDisk has announced price increases and is now requiring full payment, indicating a shift in the storage market towards a seller's cycle, which may pose challenges for buyers as prices rise.

- **Emerging Trend in Commodity Stockpiling**: The trend of stockpiling commodities is gaining traction, driven by a "strong security" logic that is reshaping metal valuations, as investors prioritize the security of physical assets amid geopolitical tensions and economic uncertainties.

- **US Dollar Decline Continues**: The US dollar has experienced two consecutive years of decline, a trend viewed as a historical pattern, with 1995 cited as a key reference point, suggesting potential implications for investors and the broader economy.

- **AI Data Centers Face Electricity Shortage**: An ongoing "electricity shortage" is impacting AI data centers, with some facing a seven-year wait for grid connections. In response, Elon Musk has increased orders for gas turbines to meet the energy demands of these facilities.
