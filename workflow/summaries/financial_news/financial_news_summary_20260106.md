## Global Financial News

- **Goldman Sachs Bullish on Chinese Equities**: Goldman Sachs has recommended overweighting Chinese equities, projecting an average annual increase of 15% to 20% over the next two years, signaling strong growth potential in the Chinese stock market.

- **Zijin Mining Reaches Market Milestone**: Zijin Mining's market value has surpassed one trillion yuan, with a projected full-year net profit of up to 52 billion yuan. Citi has dismissed concerns regarding the founder's resignation as unfounded.

- **Surge in Intelligent Driving Stocks**: Hong Kong stocks related to intelligent driving concepts surged following NVIDIA's launch of its first open-source inference VLA model, which is expected to advance secure autonomous driving technologies.

- **Momentum in AI Healthcare Stocks**: AI healthcare concept stocks in the Hong Kong market are gaining traction as major companies make strategic moves, with institutional investors optimistic about the industry's growth potential.

- **Rapid Rise of Securities Firms**: Shares of Hong Kong-listed securities firms surged, led by Guotai Junan International, which saw gains of over 12%. Other firms like Zhongjin Company and China Galaxy also experienced significant increases.

- **Technology Index Gains**: Hong Kong stocks surged, with the technology index expanding nearly 2%. Companies such as SenseTime and Hua Hong Semiconductor reported gains of over 6%.

- **LME Copper Prices Hit New Highs**: LME copper prices continue to reach new highs, positively impacting Hong Kong-listed copper stocks. Institutions attribute this rise to supply shortages and regional mismatches.

- **NVIDIA's New Rubin Platform**: NVIDIA has launched the new Rubin platform, with shipments expected in the second half of the year. This development coincides with Goldman Sachs' bullish outlook on Chinese equities amidst signs of a tightening labor market in the US.
