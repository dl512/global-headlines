## Global Financial News

- **European Defense Stocks Rally**: European defense stocks surged amid rising geopolitical tensions, particularly following US President Donald Trump's proposal for a 50% increase in military spending, projected to reach $1.5 trillion by 2027. The Stoxx Europe Aerospace and Defense index rose by 1.4%, with Leonardo shares increasing by 3.1%.

- **Market Declines in Hong Kong and US**: Recent market turbulence has led to declines in both Hong Kong and US stocks, with all three major indexes falling approximately 1%. Goldman Sachs asserts that the bull market continues, although momentum is weakening, as technology stocks like Meituan and Baidu dropped over 3%.

- **Economic Outlook for 2026**: Analysts predict strong economic resilience in the US, with the MAG7 (the seven largest tech companies) expected to continue leading the stock market. Additionally, three 'under-the-radar sectors' may present opportunities for excess returns, although specifics were not provided.

- **Gray Market Insights on Biotech and AI Companies**: Ruibo Biotech, a biopharmaceutical company, closed over 34% higher in the gray market, earning HKD 4,006 per lot. Similarly, MINIMAX-WP, a leading AI large model company, surged 24.61%, earning HKD 812 per lot.

- **Jinxun Resources Performance**: Jinxun Resources, a high-quality cathode copper manufacturer, saw its stock close 29% higher, yielding a profit of HKD 1,740 per lot, reflecting strong market performance.

- **MIIT Warns on Lithium Battery Competition**: The Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT) in China issued a warning about irrational competition in the lithium battery industry, involving over ten leading companies, and called for regulatory measures to ensure fair competition.

- **Sinopec and China National Aviation Fuel Restructuring**: Sinopec and China National Aviation Fuel Group announced a restructuring plan approved by the State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission, although details on the implications were not specified.

- **Taiwan Semiconductor Target Price Increase**: Wall Street has raised Taiwan Semiconductor's target price significantly, anticipating a revenue growth of 30% by 2026, amidst the broader market turbulence affecting both Hong Kong and US stocks.

- **Debut of Hard Tech Companies in Hong Kong**: Hong Kong-listed hard tech companies Jingfeng Medical and TianShu ZhiXin had strong debuts, driven by supportive policies, highlighting the growing interest in the sector.
