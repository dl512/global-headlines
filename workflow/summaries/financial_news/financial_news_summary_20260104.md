## Global Financial News

- **Xiaomi's Livestream Response**: Lei Jun addressed ten major criticisms of Xiaomi during a four-hour livestream, emphasizing the need for objective criticism. He also announced that Xiaomi Auto aims to deliver 550,000 vehicles by 2026.

- **Hong Kong Stock Market Recovery**: Analysts predict a recovery in risk appetite for the Hong Kong stock market, recommending investors focus on high-growth emerging industries. Recent H-share IPOs performed strongly, with Biren Technology and Insilico Medicine surging nearly 76% and over 56%, respectively.

- **Decline in Global Electric Vehicle Sales**: A significant slump in electric vehicle sales growth in Europe and America is expected, reaching its lowest point since the pandemic. This downturn reflects broader market challenges for the electric vehicle industry.

- **U.S. Intervention in Venezuela**: Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro claims a "flash-attack" by the U.S. amid rising tensions, which includes a maritime blockade causing oil tankers to bypass Venezuela. This situation raises concerns about the management of Venezuela's substantial Bitcoin assets, estimated at $600 billion.

- **Impact of U.S. Actions on Oil Markets**: The potential U.S. intervention in Venezuela may leave global crude oil markets largely unaffected, indicating resilience in oil prices despite geopolitical tensions.

- **Federal Reserve's Interest Rate Outlook**: The Federal Reserve's stance on interest rate cuts suggests that any potential reductions will depend on economic conditions and are likely postponed until the second half of the year, reflecting a cautious approach to monetary policy.
