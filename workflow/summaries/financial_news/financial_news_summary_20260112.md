## Global Financial News

- **Fed Chair Powell Under Investigation**: Federal Reserve Chairman Jerome Powell is facing a federal criminal investigation concerning a $2.5 billion renovation of the Fed's headquarters, which he claims is linked to US President Donald Trump's dissatisfaction with interest rate decisions.

- **Kuaishou's AI Product Boosts Stock**: Kuaishou's stock has risen significantly as its AI product, Ke Ling, gains popularity overseas during the New Year, with analysts projecting substantial revenue growth for fiscal year 2026.

- **China's Satellite Ambitions**: China has submitted applications for an additional 200,000 satellites, resulting in a rise in commercial aerospace stocks in Hong Kong, as institutional investors express optimism about the sector's growth potential.

- **AI Stocks Surge in Hong Kong**: The majority of AI concept stocks in the Hong Kong market have risen amid recent developments in the AI industry, with institutions optimistic about the acceleration of commercialization for these companies.

- **SenseTime Gains from Investment**: SenseTime's stock price increased following a nearly RMB100 million strategic investment from Henan Huirong, expected to enhance the company's growth in the technology sector.

- **Google Leads AI Advertising**: Google has introduced advertisements within its AI platform, Gemini, marking a significant advancement in the anticipated AI business model and potentially reshaping digital advertising.

- **Spot Gold Hits Record High**: Spot gold prices have surged to $4,600 for the first time, reflecting a cumulative increase of approximately $280 in January, highlighting its status as a safe-haven asset amid economic uncertainties.

- **Trump Encourages Investment in Venezuela**: US President Donald Trump has urged oil companies to invest tens of billions of dollars in Venezuela, assuring them of "absolute safety" in their investments amidst the country's economic challenges.

- **OmniVision Group's IPO Performance**: OmniVision Group, a semiconductor design company, opened its IPO more than 3% higher on its debut, earning HKD 320 per lot, signaling positive market interest.

- **US Earnings Season and Economic Indicators**: The upcoming "Super Week" is set to test Wall Street's confidence in the bull market, with key economic indicators like the Consumer Price Index (CPI) and Producer Price Index (PPI) expected to significantly impact market sentiment.
