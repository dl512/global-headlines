## Global Financial News

- **Hong Kong Stock Market Fluctuates**: The Hong Kong stock market showed slight fluctuations at midday, with the Technology Index decreasing by 0.06%. Sectors such as brain-computer interfaces and commercial aerospace were active, notably with Lens Technology surging over 10%.

- **US President Donald Trump Directs Bond Purchases**: US President Donald Trump has ordered Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac to purchase $200 billion in bonds, a move likened to quantitative easing aimed at stimulating the economy amid ongoing economic challenges.

- **RVMD Surges Amid Acquisition Talks**: Cancer drug developer RVMD saw a surge in after-hours trading following reports of acquisition talks with Merck & Co., potentially valued at $32 billion, indicating strong interest in the company's future.

- **Supply-Side Risks Push Oil Prices Higher**: Supply-side risks have driven oil prices to a two-week high, benefiting Hong Kong-listed oil and gas equipment stocks, which have continued to see gains amid rising market conditions.

- **Commercial Aerospace Sector Growth in Guangzhou**: Guangzhou is intensifying its efforts in the commercial aerospace sector, with enterprises across the industry chain set to benefit from this growth, reflecting ongoing enthusiasm for commercial aerospace concepts.

- **Goldman Sachs Raises Copper Price Target**: Copper prices have surged unexpectedly, prompting Goldman Sachs to raise its target price for the first half of the year while maintaining a cautious outlook due to potential corrections following US tariffs.

- **Lens Technology Stock Rises**: Lens Technology's Hong Kong stock rose following Qiangnao Technology's confirmation of completing approximately 2 billion yuan in financing, highlighting the company's role in mass production of core hardware modules.

- **Bull Market in Metals May End Abruptly**: Analysts predict an abrupt end to the current bull market in metals, particularly gold, silver, and copper, signaling potential challenges for investors as market conditions shift significantly this year.
