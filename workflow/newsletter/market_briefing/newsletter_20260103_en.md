## Regulatory Announcements

- **Baidu Plans Spin-Off of AI Chip Subsidiary Kunlunxin**: Baidu, Inc. has announced its intention to spin off its subsidiary Kunlunxin, a provider of AI computing chips, and seek a separate listing on the Main Board of the Hong Kong Stock Exchange. A confidential listing application was submitted on January 1, 2026. The spin-off will involve a public offering in Hong Kong and a placement to institutional investors, although specific details are still pending. Baidu has received a waiver from the requirement to provide existing shareholders with assured entitlement to Kunlunxin shares, citing excessive costs and regulatory burdens.
