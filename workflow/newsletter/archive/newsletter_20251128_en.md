### Guinea-Bissau Coup Sends Shockwaves Through West Africa

A significant political crisis has erupted in Guinea-Bissau as armed forces have arrested President Embaló and closed the country's borders. According to [The Point](https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/bissau-armed-forces-arrest-embalo-opposition-leader-close-borders) in Gambia, military forces detained the opposition leader amid escalating tensions. [Le Soleil](https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/politique/crise-en-guinee-bissau-le-president-dechu-embalo-exfiltre-et-accueilli-a-dakar/) from Senegal reports that President Embaló has been safely exfiltrated to Dakar following his ousting.

The regional implications of this coup are significant, with [Goobjoog News](https://goobjoog.com/2025/11/28/madaxweynihii-la-afgambiyay-ee-guinea-bissau-oo-gaaray-dalka-senegal/) in Somalia also covering the arrival of the deposed president in Senegal. This marks another concerning instance of political instability in West Africa, a region that has experienced several coups in recent years.

International observers are monitoring the situation closely, as the political vacuum could have far-reaching consequences for regional security. The Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS) is expected to respond to this development, which threatens democratic governance in the region.

### Hong Kong Tower Fire Claims 94 Lives in Devastating Tragedy

A catastrophic fire in Hong Kong has resulted in at least 94 deaths, with hundreds still missing as rescue operations continue. [BBC News](https://www.bbc.com/news/live/c2emg1kj1klt) reports that emergency teams are searching through towers for survivors, in what appears to be one of the deadliest fires in the city's history.

The disaster has garnered international attention, with [The Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/asia/news/hong-kong-nears-end-search-and-rescue-mission-tower-fire-toll-rises-94-4045596) from Bangladesh noting that search and rescue operations are nearing completion as the confirmed death toll continues to rise. [Le Matin](https://www.lematin.ch/story/hong-kong-incendie-geant-le-bilan-grimpe-a-94-morts-103459239) from Morocco also reported on the mounting casualties.

The Ethiopian News Agency [ENA](https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_7800687) states that Ethiopia has officially extended condolences to China following the tragic fire, highlighting the international response to this devastating incident. Authorities are investigating the cause of the blaze while focusing on recovery efforts and supporting affected families.
