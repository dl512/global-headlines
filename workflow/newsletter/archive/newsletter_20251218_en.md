While preparations for these talks progress, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky emphasizes that Moscow appears to be gearing up for a prolonged conflict, further complicating the peace efforts. Reports from [Delfi](https://www.delfi.lt/naujienos/uzsienyje/karas-ukrainoje-jav-ir-rusijos-pareigunai-savaitgali-majamyje-surengs-taikos-derybas-120187870) chronicle Zelensky’s concerns about Russia's military intentions, casting a shadow over the potential outcomes of the Miami talks. The urgency of finding a resolution is crucial not only for regional stability but also for the lives of millions impacted by the ongoing war.
