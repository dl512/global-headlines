## In-Depth Coverage:

### Shooting Near the White House Shakes Washington

A shooting incident near the White House has sent shockwaves through Washington D.C., with multiple international sources reporting on the attack that left two National Guard members wounded. According to [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/live/2025/11/26/us/national-guard-shooting-dc), the Guard members were critically wounded in the shooting. The incident prompted immediate security responses, with authorities quickly securing the area.

President Donald Trump responded forcefully to the attack, with [Phileleftheros](https://www.philenews.com/kosmos/diethni/article/1650262/sinagermos-stin-ouasingkton-pirovolisan-andres-tis-ethnofrouras-konta-ston-lefko-iko-enas-se-krisimi-katastasi/) from Cyprus reporting that Trump referred to the arrested shooter as an "animal" who will "pay dearly." Multiple sources, including [RIA Novosti](https://ria.ru/20251126/vashington-2057853236.html) from Russia, noted that President Trump linked the incident to concerns about illegal immigration, calling undocumented migrants a threat to national security. The attack has intensified ongoing debates about immigration and security policies in the United States.

European media outlets, including [NOS](https://nos.nl/article/2592147-twee-leden-nationale-garde-neergeschoten-bij-witte-huis-toestand-kritiek) from the Netherlands and [DR](https://www.dr.dk/nyheder/udland/militaerfolk-skudt-taet-paa-det-hvide-hus) from Denmark, reported that the victims' conditions remain critical, as authorities continue to investigate the motive behind the attack. The incident has heightened security measures around government buildings in Washington D.C.

### Hong Kong High-Rise Fire Disaster Claims Dozens of Lives

A devastating fire in a Hong Kong high-rise building has resulted in a mounting death toll, with multiple sources confirming at least 44 fatalities and hundreds still missing. According to [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/international/many-killed-as-fire-rages-through-bamboo-scaffolding-at-hong-kongs-high-rise-building/article70325713.ece) from India, the blaze has left hundreds unaccounted for as rescue operations continue. [BBC News](https://www.bbc.com/news/live/c2emg1kj1klt) from the UK reports that authorities have confirmed 44 deaths with 279 people still missing.

The Philippine Daily Inquirer [reports](https://cebudailynews.inquirer.net/673461/update-3-arrested-in-hong-kong-as-high-rise-fire-leaves-44-dead) that three individuals have been arrested in connection with the fire, suggesting potential criminal elements in the tragedy. Chinese President Xi Jinping has extended condolences and called for comprehensive rescue efforts, according to [Xinhua News Agency](https://english.news.cn/20251127/db4876cc062c44629f37b94a262a2fe5/c.html). The fire has destroyed the homes of thousands, with [The Straits Times](https://www.straitstimes.com/asia/east-asia/couldnt-do-anything-deadly-hong-kong-fire-devours-homes-of-thousands) from Singapore describing the devastating impact on residents.

Emergency services continue to battle the aftermath, with over 800 firefighters involved in the operation as reported by [24sata](https://www.24sata.hr/news/fotografije-uzasa-u-hong-kongu-vise-od-800-vatrogasaca-bori-se-s-vatrenom-stihijom-1090788?24sata_ref=frontpage-home) from Croatia. The cause of the fire remains under investigation as authorities work to provide aid to those affected by the disaster.

### Trump's Ukraine Peace Plan Creates Global Tensions

President Donald Trump's approach to the Ukraine conflict continues to generate significant international coverage, with various perspectives on his peace proposal efforts. According to [Le Monde](https://www.lemonde.fr/en/opinion/article/2025/11/26/trump-s-temptation-to-abandon-ukraine-casts-munich-shadow-over-the-white-house_6747862_23.html) from France, Trump's apparent willingness to reduce support for Ukraine has raised concerns about potential appeasement, drawing comparisons to the Munich Agreement of the late 1930s.

The Kyiv Independent [reports](https://kyivindependent.com/witkoff-says-he-has-space-to-reach-ukraine-russia-peace-deal-zelensky-to-visit-white-house-bloomberg-reports/) that a secret recording revealed Steve Witkoff, reportedly acting as an intermediary, promised Russian aides he could influence President Trump and pressure Ukraine into accepting a peace deal involving concessions related to Donetsk and possible land swaps. According to [Liberation](https://www.liberation.fr/international/amerique/negociations-sur-lukraine-steve-witkoff-lhomme-qui-murmurait-a-loreille-du-kremlin-20251126_7H22QM4RWRHTXBRB2DDEFUTPCE/) from France, Witkoff has been described as "the man who whispered in the Kremlin's ear" regarding Ukraine negotiations.

Meanwhile, [Sky News](https://news.sky.com/story/ukraine-war-latest-russia-ukrainian-drones-massive-attack-putin-zelenskyy-trump-12541713) reports on the Kremlin's response to Trump's statements on nuclear weapons, adding further complexity to the diplomatic situation. The recent meeting between Trump and Ukrainian President Zelensky appears to have been tense, with [The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/europe/ukraine-russia-war-live-poland-nato-trump-zelensky-putin-b2845715.html) noting that European leaders are eager to show Ukraine that it can still count on their support despite uncertainty about the US position.

President Trump has taken additional measures related to the conflict, including imposing sanctions on two of Russia's largest oil companies, which [The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/europe/ukraine-russia-war-trump-zelensky-putin-meeting-live-b2848952.html) reports has led to a 5.6% jump in oil prices. Former Russian president Dmitry Medvedev described these sanctions as an "act of war" against Russia, highlighting the escalating tensions.

### Military Coup in Guinea-Bissau Destabilizes West Africa

A military coup in Guinea-Bissau has upended the country's political order, with soldiers declaring "total control" of the nation. According to [A Nação](https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2025/11/26/golpe-de-estado-na-guine-bissau-militares-declaram-controle-total-do-pais-em-actualizacao/) from Cabo Verde, the military has suspended upcoming elections and removed President Embaló from power. [L'Essor](https://lessor.ml/posts/guinee-bissau-prise-de-pouvoir-par-des-militaires-et-suspension-des-elections-69272b8c9213b) from Mali confirms the suspension of elections following the military takeover.

The coup has prompted international reactions, with [Goobjoog News](https://goobjoog.com/2025/11/26/militariga-guinea-bissau-oo-sheegay-in-ay-xilka-ka-rideen-madaxweyne-embalo/) from Somalia reporting that the military claims to have officially removed President Embaló from office. Ghana has strongly condemned the coup, with [Joy News](https://www.myjoyonline.com/ghana-blasts-guinea-bissau-coup-demands-immediate-return-to-constitutional-rule/) reporting that the Ghanaian government is calling for an immediate return to constitutional rule.

The situation in Guinea-Bissau adds to a troubling pattern of military interventions in West African governments, raising concerns about regional stability and democratic governance. [Público](https://www.publico.pt/2025/11/26/mundo/noticia/golpe-guinebissau-eleicoes-suspensas-presidente-demitido-fronteiras-media-encerrados-2156173) from Portugal reports that in addition to suspending elections and dismissing the President, the coup leaders have closed borders and media outlets, further isolating the country during this period of political upheaval.

Similarly, [Kathimerini](https://www.kathimerini.gr/society/563942959/leipsydria-se-katastasi-ektaktis-anagkis-leros-kai-patmos-tin-pempti-i-apofasi-gia-attiki/) from Greece reports water shortages in Leros, Patmos, and Attica, indicating a wider pattern of water scarcity in the Mediterranean region.

Climate change impacts are particularly evident in the Pacific, where the [Solomon Star](https://www.solomonstarnews.com/rising-tide-push-gizos-fishing-village-to-the-edge/) reports that rising sea levels are pushing Gizo's fishing village "to the edge," threatening both livelihoods and community infrastructure. In Malaysia, [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2025/11/27/flood-victims-dip-in-kelantan-rise-in-five-other-states) reports that while flood victim numbers have decreased in Kelantan, they have increased in five other states, illustrating the ongoing challenge of managing extreme weather events.

These environmental challenges are occurring against a backdrop of policy changes, with [Vanuatu Daily Post](https://www.dailypost.vu/news/usaid-cuts-trigger-concerns-for-key-health-programs/article_ff97e194-51bd-5c5c-a51d-4a0bc0669d9d.html) reporting concerns that cuts to USAID funding could impact essential health programs, potentially limiting resources available to address climate-related health issues in vulnerable regions.
