### Global Cancer Care Advancements Focus on Regional Access

Significant developments in cancer care accessibility are emerging across multiple countries as governments prioritize bringing treatment closer to patients. According to [New Vision](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/health/govt-to-recruit-more-medics-for-regional-canc-NV_223607), Uganda plans to recruit more medical professionals for regional cancer centers, aiming to improve treatment availability beyond major urban areas.

This initiative builds on earlier efforts outlined by [cancerworld.net](https://cancerworld.net/regional-cancer-centres-to-boost-access-to-cancer-care-across-uganda/), which reported that regional cancer centers are being established to boost access across Uganda. The [IAEA](https://www.iaea.org/newscenter/news/jordan-inaugurates-new-pet-ct-bringing-cancer-care-closer-to-more-patients) also reports that Jordan has inaugurated a new PET-CT scanner at Al-Bashir Hospital, significantly enhancing diagnostic capabilities and reducing the need for patients to travel abroad for advanced tests.

Jordanian Minister of Health Firas Al-Hawari emphasized that the introduction of this technology "will reduce the need for patients to travel abroad for such advanced tests, saving time, effort and costs for patients and their families." Similar efforts are underway in Nigeria, as the [IAEA](https://www.iaea.org/newscenter/news/nigeria-makes-strides-in-cancer-control-views-decentralization-of-services-to-expand-access) reports that the country is making strides in cancer control through decentralization of services to expand access.
